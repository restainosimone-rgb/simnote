The quickest way is from the app: Settings → Send feedback or report a problem (on Mac also from the Help menu). You can describe the problem and attach up to 3 screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my notes saved?

On your device, or in the SimNote folder of your iCloud Drive if you turn on sync in Settings → Storage & sync.

How do I sync Mac and iPhone?

On both devices choose iCloud in Settings → Storage & sync, with the same Apple Account.

What is the difference between Board and Notes?

They are two ways to look at the same archive: the Board shows everything on one page, the Notes view is made for writing one note after another. The "Welcome to SimNote" note explains it step by step.

I deleted a note by mistake

Deleted notes stay in Recently deleted for the time chosen in Settings → Trash.

Can I bring my notes from another app?

Yes: Settings → Import accepts text and Markdown files, and on Mac also Apple Notes. You can also export everything as .md files.

How do I see the welcome tour again?

On Mac: Help menu → Board tour. The tutorial notes can be recreated from Help → Tutorial notes.

Is SimNote free?

Yes, with no ads and no subscriptions.
