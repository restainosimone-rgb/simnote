Privacy Policy

Support Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

  • Your notes stay on your device or in your iCloud Drive. I cannot read them.
  • No accounts, no ads, no usage analytics, no tracking.
  • I only receive data if you choose to send feedback from the app.

Who is responsible

SimNote is developed by Simone Restaino ("SR"), Italy. For any privacy question, write to simnoteofficial@gmail.com.

Your notes

Notes, folders, canvases, PDFs and attachments are saved on your device or, if you turn on sync, in the SimNote folder of your iCloud Drive. Sync is handled by Apple under its privacy policy. The developer has no access to this data and never receives it.

Face ID and app lock

If you turn on the lock, recognition happens entirely on your device through Apple. SimNote only receives the result (success or failure), never biometric data.

Photos

When you attach a photo, the app only receives the images you select. It does not access the rest of your library.

Feedback and bug reports

If you use "Send feedback", the following is sent:

  • the text you write and the message type (problem, idea, other);
  • the images you choose to attach (up to 3);
  • your email, only if you enter it, so I can reply;
  • if you leave the option on: app version, device model, system version and language.

The message goes through a Google service (Google Apps Script) and arrives as an email in the developer's Gmail inbox. It is used only to reply and to improve the app, it is never shared, and it is deleted on request. Legal basis: your consent, given by sending the message.

How long data is kept

Feedback messages stay in the inbox as long as they are needed to handle the report, and no longer than 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete the messages you sent, by writing to the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Children

SimNote does not knowingly collect data from children. Sending feedback is always optional.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
